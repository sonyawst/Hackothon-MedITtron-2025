## 🎯 Overview

Oncology Therapy Optimizer addresses the critical challenge of personalized cancer treatment by providing physicians with a web-based tool to simulate and optimize chemotherapy regimens for individual patients.

**Problem**: Standard chemotherapy protocols don't account for individual patient variability, leading to:
- ❌ Suboptimal tumor suppression
- ❌ Severe side effects  
- ❌ Drug resistance development
- ❌ Increased healthcare costs

**Our Solution**: A mathematical modeling platform that:
- ✅ Simulates tumor dynamics and drug pharmacokinetics
- ✅ Optimizes dosage and scheduling
- ✅ Predicts resistance development
- ✅ Provides data-driven recommendations

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔬 Scientific Foundation
- **Differential Equation Models**: ODE-based simulation of tumor growth
- **Resistance Modeling**: Compartmental models for sensitive/resistant cells
  In the future:
- **Pharmacokinetics**: Drug concentration dynamics over time

### 💻 User Experience
- **Web Interface**: Physician-friendly dashboard
- **Interactive Visualizations**: Real-time simulation results
- **Treatment History**: Patient case tracking
- **Export Capabilities**: Report generation

### 🔒 Security & Privacy
- **Data Anonymization**: Hash-based patient identification
- **GDPR/152-ФЗ Compliance**: Medical data protection
